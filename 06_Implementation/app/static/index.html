      Encoding library
      
      
      Eighteen ways to load a window into six qubits: three circuit families, three bandwidths, two depths. Expressibility is the KL divergence from Haar-random states (lower is more expressive); entangling is the mean Meyer-Wallach measure.
      
    
      
    
      
    
      
  
      

  
  
      
    
      
      
      Kernel lab
      
      
      A quantum kernel is the overlap of two encoded states. Each matrix compares 60 normal windows with 60 anomalous ones: a useful kernel has a dark normal block and a pale cross block.

      How Q-DRIFT works
      
      
      One loop runs for every telemetry window. The quantum model is small and cheap to refit; choosing an encoding, the expensive part, happens only when the data’s behaviour changes.
